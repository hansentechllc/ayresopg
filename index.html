01 / About AOPG

We help leaders identify and overcome the barriers that stand between their organizations and sustained high performance.

AOPG partners with leaders to build organizations where people can succeed, excellence is expected, and high performance can be sustained.

We help identify the root causes of underperformance, create deliberate frameworks for improvement, and strengthen leaders’ capacity and courage to drive meaningful, lasting change.

04 / Leadership

Experience Provides Perspective

Dr. Deb Ayres brings five decades of experience in education, organizational leadership, human resources, and executive leadership to AOPG. Her career has spanned roles from teacher and principal to doctoral faculty member, department chair, and senior vice president of human resources, working with governing boards, executives, managers, and employees across organizations.

Deb views organizations as interconnected systems where people, leadership, structure, processes, technology, and resources must work together. She helps leaders look beyond immediate challenges to identify underlying conditions, strengthen organizational foundations, develop high-performing teams, and create frameworks for sustained improvement.
