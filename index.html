03 / Our Approach

How We Work

Performance gaps exist in most organizations and high performing departments or units don’t happen by accident. They are built by leaders who are able and willing to see clearly what’s working, what isn’t, and what needs to change.

AOPG’s approach is to help leaders untangle what is causing the gaps between current and desired performance. Our first step is seeing the whole and complex picture clearly.

AOPG helps examines organizational performance through six dimensions:

  • Foundational and Operational Infrastructure
  • Organizational Structure and Alignment
  • Operational and Business Processes
  • Leadership Capacity
  • Employee Performance Assessment, Improvement, Development, and Management
  • Policies and Practices

Performance problems rarely exist neatly within a single dimension. The problem may be rooted in the unit's structure, employee performance, leadership, processes, infrastructure, or several conditions working together. Seeing the connections is essential to knowing where the work should begin.

AOPG uses a deliberate, adaptable approach to guide that work. We help our clients do the right work, in the right order, and at the right time.
