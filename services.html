02 / Services

Focused guidance when it matters most.

AOPG engagements are structured around diagnosing performance challenges, building a deliberate framework for improvement, supporting implementation, and advising leaders through difficult organizational and personnel decisions.

01

Service offering

Assessment & Framework Engagement

Diagnose the sources of underperformance, define the desired outcomes, and build a phased framework for achieving them.

AOPG helps leaders identify the causes of performance gaps, establish priorities, and build a deliberate framework for improvement—defining what needs to happen, in what order, and how success will be measured.

02

Service offering

Implementation Advising & Support

Coach and advise the leaders and employees responsible for executing the established framework.

AOPG supports those responsible for the work as they put the framework into action. Advising and coaching help leaders navigate obstacles, assess progress, make necessary adjustments, and maintain focus on the desired outcomes. The client retains responsibility for execution.

03

Service offering

Executive Advising

Work directly with leaders who need an experienced, objective advisor on organizational and personnel performance issues.

Executive advising is integrated throughout AOPG engagements and is also available as a stand-alone service. Leaders can use AOPG as a trusted partner when evaluating difficult organizational or personnel issues, considering alternatives, making decisions, or determining the best path forward.

04

Service offering

Tailored Engagements

Every AOPG engagement is tailored to your needs, priorities, and intended outcomes—never a standardized approach.

Our work is driven by the information and understanding required to responsibly define the engagement to achieve your intended outcomes; not by a standardized template applied to all circumstances. Recommended services will align with the breadth, depth, and level of engagement you request.
