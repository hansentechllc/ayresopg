01

Purpose and Scope

This Website Privacy Policy explains how Ayres Organizational Performance Group, LLC (“AOPG,” “we,” or “us”) collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit ayresopg.com (the “Site”) or submit information through the Site. The Site is designed primarily to provide information about AOPG and allow prospective clients and other visitors to contact AOPG.

02

Information You Provide to AOPG

AOPG collect personal information that you choose to provide through the Site, including information submitted through an inquiry or contact form. Depending on the form and information you choose to provide, this may include your name, preferred name, preferred salutation, email address, work or mobile phone number, organization name, job title, organization website, referral source, information about what is happening in your organization, the outcomes you are seeking, timing or urgency, preferred method or time of contact, and other information you choose to submit.

AOPG uses information you provide to respond to your inquiry, communicate with you, understand the reason for your contact, determine whether AOPG may be an appropriate professional resource, manage prospective-client communications, maintain appropriate business records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

04

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Site may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for Site operation, security, performance, or basic analytics. AOPG does not intend to use the Site to build advertising profiles or to sell personal information.

05

How AOPG Shares Information

AOPG does not sell or rent personal information for mass marketing purposes. AOPG may share personal information with service providers that perform services on AOPG’s behalf, such as website hosting, data storage, information technology, security, email, or other business-support services. Such providers receive information only as reasonably necessary to perform those services.

AOPG may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process; protect the rights, property, security, or safety of AOPG or others; investigate fraud or misuse; or complete a business transaction such as a merger, reorganization, or sale of substantially all business assets.

06

Third-Party Websites

The Site may contain links to websites operated by third parties. AOPG does not control those websites or their privacy practices. Visitors should review the privacy practices of a third-party website before providing information to it.
